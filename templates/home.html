How learning works

Setup takes about a minute. After that it's one short lesson at a time.

  1. Say where you're at

    Write a couple of lines, or tap through a few quick questions. There's no sign-up form.

  2. Get your roadmap

    MoBo plans 8 to 10 steps from where you are, and writes each lesson around your situation.

  3. Learn and level up

    Finishing a step earns 100 XP and unlocks the next. You can ask MoBo anything on the way.
